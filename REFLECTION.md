# Reflection

**Question:** What should be public, what should stay hidden, and what should be decided by AI versus by a human in a bounty system?

In a bounty system, raw ideas and wallet context should remain hidden at the start to prevent manipulation, then become fully transparent once the process is complete so the outcome can be audited by anyone. Public information should include the rules, the rubric, the deadlines, and the reward, meaning everything participants agreed to compete under. What stays hidden is the content of each answer until the contest closes, because visible submissions let latecomers copy and improve on rivals' work, which is exactly what the commit-reveal scheme prevents. AI excels at managing deterministic on-chain logic, enforcing rules at speed, and performing objective data verification without bias. Humans, on the other hand, bring original creativity, contextual judgment, and strategic value assessment that AI cannot replicate. For this reason, technical security and rule enforcement should be delegated to AI and smart contracts, while creative inputs, the introduction of genuine randomness, and final decisions should remain under human control. This balance produces a fair and manipulation-resistant agentic economy that is both machine-enforced and human-centered.
